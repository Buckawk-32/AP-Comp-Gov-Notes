## What is Globalization?
- -> *interconnectedness* of the world's economies, cultures, and populations, brought about by cross-border trade in goods and services, technology, and flows of investment, people, and information
    - *Economic globalization* has led to the growth of international trade
        - Restructing of economies through Multinational companies
        - Higher exchange of ideas and values


## Economic Globalization
- *Benefits*
    - Deepened cross-national connections
        - Boosted global economic growth
        - Provided international customers for businesses

- *Challegenes*
    - Hurts domestic industry
        - *Comparative advantage* -> an economy's ability to produce a particular good or service at a lower oppurtunity than its trading partners
    - Economic dependency
        - Ex. Great Depression
    - Wealth inequality
    - Homogenous world
    - Enviromental degradation


## Economic Liberalization
- -> the process of removing government intervention in trade, investment, and other movements of capital
    - Liberalization increases the economy, which will increase legitimacy

## How did globalization and liberalization occur?
- *1st Cause: New Technologies*
    - Transportation -> shipping, aircraft
        - Ex. Caravelle and Nao ships in 15th century Protugal to now
        - Ex. First flight in 1903 (Wright Brothers) to now
    - Internet & communication
    - Have led to:
        - Greater exchanges of ideas and information
        - More efficieny 
        - Higher profts for companies and nation-states

- *2nd Cause: Rise of Multinational Corporations (MNCs)*
    - -> company that owns and controls the production of good or services in at least one country other than its home country
    - Why operate in multiple countries?
        - Cheaper labor, more access to raw materials, and flexible laws
    - Often conflict with countries' domestic policies
        - Ex. Labor, enviroment, taxation, etc.

- *3rd Cause: Immigration*
    - Labor market & expansion
        - Allows businesses to access a larfer and more diverse workforce
    - Cultural & economic integration
        - Actively engaged in the interchange of languages, culture and ideas
    - FDI & remittances
        - Strengthens economic ties between countries

- *4th Cause: International Organization (IOs)*
    - Especially financial organizations
    - Major IOs
        - [IMF](#international-monetary-fund-imf)
        - [World Bank](#world-bank)
        - [WTO](#world-trade-organization-wto)

### International Monetary Fund (IMF)
- -> provides financial assistance, policy advice, and technical suport to member countries to promote economic stability, reduce poverty, and facilitate international trade and monetary cooperation
    - 191 members

### World Bank
- -> provides loans, grants, and technical assistance to developing countries to reduce poverty, promote economic development, and support infrastructure, education, healthcare, and toher critical sectors
    - 189 members

### World Trade Organization (WTO)
- -> regulates global trade by establishing trade rules, resolving disputes between member countries and promoting free and fair trade through negotiations and agreements to facilitate economic growth and stability
    - 166 members


## Globalization <-> Economic Liberalization
>[!NOTE] 
> Remember: the 2 are connected
- Economic liberalization has made it easier for states to buy and sell goods across borders
    - By removing obstacles to trade, nations begin to rely more on each other for imports and exports, consqueantly contributing to ther process of globalization
- Examples:
    - Lower tariffs
    - Free trade agreements


## Conflicts of Globalization & Liberalization
- Conflicts between states and MNCs
    - MNCs often infringe on domestic laws and labor regulations
    - Disregard for the enviroment
    - Somtimes they become so powerful that they create their own laws

- Inequality
    - Some indivduals may enjoy more economic freedom than others
        - Social class, racial, gender

- Demands by civil society groups
    - Economic liberalization has come with a cost to the enviroment and labor which directly affects people's lives
    - Civil society groups have become even more important to advocate for government intervention and comprehensive policies that combat these negative impacts

- Students and disenfranchised groups
    - Rise up to demand the government defend their rights
    - Sometimes they become more nationalistic
        - They want outside industries to leave, to then support domestic industries
        - Rise of alt-right movements around the world
