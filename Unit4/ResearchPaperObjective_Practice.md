# Research Objective

## Research Question:
- How do different Authoritative Ideaologies affect electoral systems to create differences in both populations' efficacy?

- Hypothesis: More strict Authoritative Ideaologies creates lower measured population efficacy, while more free or covert Ideaologies will create higher measure population efficacy.
    - Independent Variable: Authoritative Ideaologies
    - Mechanism of Change: Electoral Systems
    - Dependent variable: populations' efficacy
