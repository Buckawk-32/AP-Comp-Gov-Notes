#  Political Legitimacy - Sources and Sustaining

## Learning Objectives
1. Describe the sources of political legitimacy for different types of regimes amoung course countries
2. Explain how govenrments maintains legitimacy


## What is Political Legitmacy?
Whether a government's constituents believe their government has right to the power they have
- Legitmacy confers authority
    - As a source of power, thus with legitmacy you gain more power
    - Ex. Source of Power that legitmacy give
        - Popular Support (More Legitmacy = More Popular Support)


## Sources of Legitmacy
What gives a government legitimacy in the first place?
- Consitutions
- Popular Elections
- Political Party Endorsement
- Effective/efficient governing
- Consistent Economic Growth
- Nationalism
- Tradition
- Ideology
- Religous Heritage


## Maintaining That Legitimacy
How do they maintain that legitimacy?
- Policy effectiveness
- Political efficacy -> belief that you understand politics and have the ability to change government
- Charismatic Leadership


## Losing Legitimacy
What makes people lose faith that their government is legitimate?
- Lack of rule of law
- Electoral Fraud
- Economic stagnation or contraction
- Growing socio-cultural divides
- Overt censorship and propaganda
- Inability to deal with natural disasters or foreign threats
