## Demographic Changes
- -> refers to shifts in the characters of a population over time
    - Effects on the cultural, poltitical, social norms of a country
    - Ex.
        - Growing/declining populations
        - Changing land use and values
        - Population movements 
        - Ethnic rvial repeace

## Demographic Changes in China
- 4 quick waves of industrialization
    - Resulted in rapid urbanization
        - Massive cities -> Shanghai (25 million), Beijing (21 million), ChongQing (15 million)

- Hukou System
    - Meant to help control population distrubtion and overcrowding
        - Rural or urban designation determines access to services

- 1 Child Policy (1979 ~ 2015)
    - Skewed gender ratio due to a preference for male children
        - Rural exceptions allowed a second child if the first was a girl
    - Resulted in gender imbalance
        - Global natural birth ratio: 105 boys per 100 girls
        - China: 121 boys per 100 girls


## Brain Drain in Iran & Nigeria
- Brain drain -> emigration of highly skilled or educated individuals from one place to another, often in seach of better job opportunities, higher salaries, improved living conditions, or greater political and economic stability
- Iran
    - Educated seek economic opportunities aboard
    - Also worry about a return to military rule
    - Religious/ethnic minorities


## Demographic Changes in Mexico
- Why move from South to North?
    - Poor economy
        - NAFTA & USMCA boosted industry but hurt Mexican farmers
        - Reason: removal of agriculutral subsidies
        - Maquiladora zones, FDI primarily in the North
    - Lack of job opportunities

    - Persecution
        - Cartels & gang violence = less protection in rural areas

    - Lack of educational opportunities
    - Making their way to the USA

- Total Fertility Rate
    - 7 in 1960
    - 1.8 in 2022
    - Primary Reason: fewer kids needed in industrialized societies
        - Plus, they are expensive

- Mexican government responses:
    - *Prospera* - programs which provide cash transfers, healthcare, and education to poor families
    - Migrant Protection Protocols provide temporary shelter to migrant workers


## Nigeria with a High Birth Rate
- Children = sign of prosperity in Nigeria
    - Religious beliefs promote large families
    - Used for labor in rural areas
    - High infant mortality rates
        - More children = more chance that some will survive to adulthood
    - Limited access to family planning and contraceptive services

- Associatd problems
    - Increased demands from growing population = straining services and infastructure
    - Youth unemployment = 30%
    - Food insecurity


## Demographic Changes in the UK
- Increased immigration
    - -> increased social and political tensions
    - Correlated with Brexit
    - Rise of far right parties
        - UK Independence Party (UKIP) led by Nigel Farange

- Aging population
    - Straining healthcare system & increased taxes


## Iran's Birth Rate Coaster
- Government can't decide what it wants
    - 1980s => encouraged high birth rates; country was at war
    - 1990s ~ 2000s => encouraged smaller families to protect social services
    - 2020s ~ none => encourages larger families to speed up Crude Birth Rate
        - Offering cash payments to families after having children
        - Maternity & paternity leave
