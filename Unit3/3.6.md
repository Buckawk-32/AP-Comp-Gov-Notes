## Example of UK Political Participation (Equality and Human Rights Commision)
- Promotions:
    - Free Fair Elections
        - Which are compeitive (vote for your seat in the legislature)

    - Referenda are common, civil society is well-established

    - Actions set by July 2025 for improved voter turnout
        1. Permit the use of bank cards as valid voter ID
        2. Introducing a digital Voter Authority Certificate
        3. Extending time one can remain anoymous without having to reapply

    - There is Right to Assembly, but action has been inconsistent
        - Public nussiances or "serious harm" to the public can lead to arrest

    - Goverment is consistent in the way it grants civil liberties, pushing for more inclusivity with the public and policy making

- Restrictions:
    - 18 or over to vote


## UK Political Participation: Assessment (The Electoral Commision)
- Regime affects very little of the political participation, making sure the more people have the agency to vote
- Although people are happy with the new processes, they are still distrustful of the institutions
    - Still there is high confidence in UK elections
- 8/10


## Example of Russia Political Participation (PONARIS Eurasia)
- Promotions:
    - Direct elections for Duma and President

- Restrictions:
    - 2012: criminalization of mass gatherings
    - Chenchen leaders are directly appointed by Putin because of their "protests":
        - Ramzan Kadyrov

    - Although there is massive electoral fraud in the government
        - Russians aren't too concerned on voting to get rid of this corruption
        - The public are pretty passive to the the fraud
    - Because Russians also generally like putin (Charmastic Leadership), they voluntarliy comply with his fraud and corruption, as long as he's in charge

    - 18, Citizenship, Mandatory to Vote, Local Government Authority


## Russia Political Participation: Assessment
- Because political participation is controlled on the electoral level, citizens don't have agency
    - But even though they vote, they are still happy if Putin is elected
- 3/10


## Example of Mexico Political Participation 
- Promotions:
    - Direct elections of the President and Legislature (some first-past-the-post, some with proportional representation)

- Restrictions:
    - Only citizens over 18
    - Must have a voter ID card to vote

## Example of Nigeria Political Participation
- Promotions:
    - Direct elections of the President and Legislature (uses first-past-the-post, single member districts)
    - Constitution protects the right for protests and peaceful assemblies

- Restrictions:
    - Only citizens over 18 can vote

## Example of Iran Political Participation
- Promotions:
    - Direct elections of the President, majles, and Assembly of Experts
        - Remember: Assemly of Experts appoints the Supreme Leader

- Restricitons:
    - Candidates for President and Assembly of Experts must be approved by the Guardian Council
        - Elections not competitive as a result
    - Mass protests are shut down quickly

## Example of China Political Participation
- Promotions:
    - Join the CPP!
    - Nationalistic and ideological campaigns
        - "Patrotic Education" campaigns: Encouraging loyalty to the Party and participation in state initiatives
        - Volunteerism and Public Service: Citizens are encouraged to engage in community projects and public welfare activites

- Restrictions:
    - Pro-democracy protests are highly suppressed 
    - Only the party can participate in government
