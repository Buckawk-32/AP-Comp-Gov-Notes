# Democracy vs Authoritarianism

## Learning Objectives
1. Describe democracy and authoritarianism
2. Compare 3 countries based on their political systems using a Venn diagram


## Democractic Regimes
- System of government in which state power is vested in the people or the general population of the state
    - Ancient Greeks: started with the Athenian Democracy
        - People rule the system, establishing the rules for the system

### Features of Democratic Regimes
- Free, fair & competitive elections
    - High citzen participation

- Transparency in government
    - UK's Corruption Perception Index

- Checks and balances within government
- Low state control on media

- Due Process Right -> to defend yourself in the court of law
- Rule -> All indivduals are equal under the law (i.e. Rule of Law)
    - But it isn't really equal, rich vs poor


## Authoritarian Regimes
- Authoritarian -> A system of government where power is invested in a small group of people who exercise that power without being constitutionally responsible to the public
    - Different types
    - First came into use in the 1850s

### Features of Authoritarian Regimes
- Opposite of the features of a Democratic Regimes


## Types of Authoritarian Regimes
1. Illiberal Democracies / Hybrid Regimes
    - Have elections, but not free, fair or competitive
    - Ex. Russia
2. One Party State
    - One political party in control
    - Ex. China, Sri Lanka somewhat...
3. Theocracy
    - Rule by religious leaders
    - Ex. Iran
4. Totalitarian Government
    - Total control over all decisions in society
    - Ex. Nazi Germany, USSR under Stalin, North Korea today
5. Military Regime / Junta
    - Ex. Myanmar (today), Chinese warlords, Nigeria used to be until 1999
