## Removal of UK Executives
- Remember: UK is a parliamentary system 
    - Fussed legislative & executive branches
    - Legislature appoints the PM

- Rmeoval of the executive is done through a confidence motion by the House of Commons
    - Vote of "no confidence" = PM resigns or calls for general election

- Last successfully used in 1979
    - James Callaghan's Labour government defeated in a no confidence vote
        - Reason: Wanted to pass a revolution referendum to give more power to Scottish parliament
    - Led to the rise of Margaret Thatcher


## Removal of Russian Executives
- Legislature has constitutional authority to impeach the President
    1. Petition for impeachment starts in the Duma (Lower House)
    2. 2/3 of members in the Duma must sign
    3. Petition is passed to the Federation Council (Upper House)
    4. Simple Majority (51%) of the Federation Council must agree to impeach
    5. If successfully impeached, the President is removed from office and the PM becomes temporary President until a mandatory election is held within 3 months

- Boris Yeltsin brought up for impeachment 3 times in the 1990s
    - All 3 attempts of impeach failed

## Removal of Iran Executive
- Supreme Leader *could be* removed by the Assembly of Experts
        - AoE => 88 Islamic scholars, chosen by the people
    - But the AoE has never done that
    - And probaly wouldn't 
        - Because half the AoE is appointed by the Ayatollah
    - Also, the process isn't really clear

- The President can be removed by the Supreme Leader or the legislature


## Removal of Nigeria Executive
- Remember: Presidential system
    - Separate executive and legislature branches
        - Balance!

- Impeachment of the President:
    - Starts from the legislature
    - 2/3 of both houses must vote to impeach if there has been "gross misconduct"
    - Vice President takes over until next election
