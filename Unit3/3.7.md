## Civil Rights vs Civil Liberties
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Liberties come from nature or God. Rights come from Law.

- [John Locke's Philosophy](/Unit2/2.3and2.4.md#lockes-political-philosophy)

- Civil Rights
    - -> protections against discrimination based on charateristics such as race, gender, or nationality (groups)
        - Ensure equal treatment for all citizens under the law
        - Usually imposed by the Legislature
        - *Group* rights that the government must protect
    - The Government must protect these rights for your
        - Needs protection *by* the government

- Civil Liberties
    - -> fundamental, natural rights and freedoms granted to indivduals at birth
        - Usually protected by a constitution or bill of rights
        - Ex. Freedom of Speech, Religion, Assembly, Press, and Privacy
        - Indivdual rights that the government can't take away
            - Needs protection *from* the government
   

## Course Countries: Liberties and Rights
- 5 protect liberties within their consitutions, 1 (UK) through common law traditions
    - Difference in te degree of protection - linear scale

### Protection of Assembly and Speech
- Protected by all 6 countries (theoretically)
    - Authoritarian => rule by law
        - -> States use the law to reinforce state power
        - Russia's 2012 law that criminalizes unapproved protests
        - Iran and China also put down protests
    - Democratic => rule of law
        - -> States and indivduals subject to the same laws
        - Nigeria: refused to pass legislation that would limit NGOs

### Freedom of the Press & Media
- Media is a *linkage institution*
    - -> organization that connects citizens with the government
        - Essentially a 4th institution (after exec., leg., judicial)
        - Bridge between people and desicion-making processes

- Democratic regimes have more media freedom
    - Freedom of Information Acts create transparency
        - But Mexico and Nigeria have trouble with officals' compliance 
    - Mix of government-owned and private media
        - Can critize government in both forms

- Authoritarian governments have no FOIA
    - Monitor and restrict media access
    - No Freedom of Information Act

#### Media Restriction Example
- China's "Great Firewall"
    - Try to search for "Tiananmen Square, 1989" or Winnie the Pooh in China

- Iran has revoked media licenses for "anti-religious" content

- Russian media has been nationalized and propaganda is highly managed
    - Journalists have weird way of dissappearning the middle of the nighht, falling out of windows, or shooting themselves in the back of the head


## Civil Liberty Scale
- The freest country in terms of civil liberties is UK because of it's consolidated democracy, consistent tradition of human rights
- The least free country in terms of civil liberties is China because of it's mass censorship and political manipulation with it's media.


## Democracy-Authoritarianism Spectrum
1. UK -> conslidated democracy
    - Not perfect, but has a long history of protecting & implementing the 7 characteristics of democracy

2. Mexico -> flawed democracy
    - Moving from authoritarianism to democracy
    - Has democratic procedures in place, but with significant restrictions on those procedures

3. Nigeria -> emerging democracy
    - Same same as Mexico

4. Russia -> Hybrid / Illiberal Regime
    - Contested elections are held, but with limited  degrees of competitiveness providing minimal civil liberty protections and governmental transparency
    - Authoritarian -> Democratic -> Authoritarian

5. Iran -> Authoritarian
    - Power is concentrated in the hands of a single indivduals, small groups of indivduals, or a party who have no rule of law to govern them

6. China -> Authoritarian
    - Same same as Iran, but better
    - Also protests are completely hidden
