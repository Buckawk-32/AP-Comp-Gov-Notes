## Political Culture and Its Influence
- Political Culture -> collective attiudes, values & beliefs of the citizenry and the norms of behavior in the political system
    - Ex: a set of beliefs among the citizens that form the foundation of the government
    - Sets expectations about the exercise of power
        - Failing to protect values = loss of legitimacy

- 3 influences on political culture:
    1. (G) Geography -> physical location and natural resources within
    2. (R) Religious traditions -> influence social norms and policy priorities
    3. (H) History -> collective history of the country

## UK Influences on Political Culture - Facts
### UK - Geography
- Small territory + limited resource = need for expansion
- Set apart from continental Europe
- No geographical barriers across Britian lead to easy transportation and communication

### UK - Religion
- Church of England established in 1534 during the protestant reformation
- Prior to English Civil War: divine right of kings

### UK - History
- Noblesse oblige -> duty of the upper classes to take reponsibillity for the welfare of the lower
- Long history of political, social, economic evolution


## UK Political Culture + Policy 
- 4 ways:
    1. G: Always destined to be seperate from Europe
    2. R: Influence of societal attitudes toward morality
    3. R: Intertwined religion and government
    4. H: Acceptance of welfare state (Ex. National Health Service)


## Mexico Influences on Political Culture - Facts
### Mexico - Geography
- Proximity to the US
    - Geographic barriers throughout the country (Ex. Mountains, Deserts)

### Mexico - Religion
- Catholicism brought over by the Spanish in the 16th Century

### Mexico - History
- Patron-clientelism -> "I scratch your back, you scratch mine."
    - Strong sense of national identity
    - Economic dependence -> Spain, USA


## Mexico Political Culture + Policy
- 3 ways:
    1. G: Regionalism & limited urbanization
    2. R: Strong catholic influence on policy (Ex. only 2/31 states allow abortion)
    3. H: Economic dependency -> both on the Northern neighbor and on government through nationalization (Ex. PEMEX oil company)

## Russia Influences on Political Culture - Facts
### Russia - Geography
- Largest country in the world
- Multi-ethnic, varied climates
- Vunerable to invasion, particularly from its west

### Russia - Religion
- Eastern Orthodox Christiantiy was central to early history
    - Russia as the "protecter of the Slavic/Orthodox" world

### Russia - History
- Rule of the Tsar & 3 pillars of the empire
    - Nationalism, orthodoxy, autocracy
- Communism & egalitarian ethos
- 2 invasions within 27 years
- Cold War
- Societal collapse in the early 1990s


## Russia Political Culture + Policy
- National pride, sovereignty, defense of the motherland
- 4 ways:
    1. G: State protects indivduals from invasion, so state is more 
    2. G: Reliance on natural resources + obession with warm water access
    3. R: No seperation of church & state
    4. H: Deep mistrust of the west & need for "buffers"


## China Influences on Poltical Culture - Facts
### China - Geography
- Developed in isolation without outside influence
- Abundant resources

### China - Religion
- Confucian ideals
    - Mandate of Heaven, fililal piety, hierarchical and harmonious social order

### China - History
- Strong ruler + strong economy = united empire
- Weak rule + weak economy = susceptitble to partition
- Century of humilation
- Han naitonalism & pride in Chinese achievements


## China Political Culture + Policy
- 3 ways:
    1. G: Sustain from within
    2. R: Social order above all else
    3. H: Han sovereignty over all Chinese (territory and other ethnicities)

## Iran Political Culture + Policy

## Politcal Culture <-> Political Socialization
- -> lifelong process of acquiring one's beliefs, values, and orientations towards the political system
    - Ex. how the political culture of a country is transmitted to the indivduals
    - Each indivdual has unique experiences that define how they view politics

- Defining factors:
    1. Political culture of a country
    2. Education & schooling
    3. Family
    4. Religion
    5. Media
    6. Socioeconomic Status
        - Middle Class -> Middle Right, leaning Conservative
        - Ultra Rich -> Starting liberal, Over time Conversative


## Political Socialization by Regime Type
> [!IMPORTANT]
> The more control a government has over the 4 defining factors, the more uniformity of thoughts exist within a state
- Authoritarian regimes directly use their agents of political socialization to inform political culture

- Democratic regimes have less work to do to keep people's endorsement of the ruling party
    - Very little pressure to socialize
