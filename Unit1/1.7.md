## Unitary System
- A system where power is concentrated at the national level and which had uniform policies
    - Local government still exist
        - But function to carry out central government policies
        - But doesn't have power to carry out state laws
    - China, Iran, UK

- Advantages:
    1. Quick Desicion Making
    2. Easier reason mangement & distrubution (for emergencies and disaters)
    3. Laws are union

- Disadvantages:
    1. Less transparency (power is all concentrated to one center)
    2. Less local voices (hard to make change as a indivdual / ignores regional problems)
    3. Minorites are rarely heard
    4. One party can dominate easily


## Federal System
- A system where power is divided among different levels of government
    - Local autonomy 
    - Federal government has reserved powers
        1. National elections (One federally determined way)
        2. Wars and Relations and Treaties 
    - Nigeria, Mexico, Russia

- Advantages:
    1. High citizen participation (because you feel like you have high efficacy)
    2. Multiple party participation
    3. Good for diverse states

- Disadvantages:
    1. Slow and inefficeint
    2. Laws are complex and will vary
    3. Constant competition for power among different levels


## Systemic Changes
- States can and do change from unitary to federal, and from federal to unitary
    - Unitary -> Federal
        - -> Devolved Unitary System
        - Central government devolves power to regional government
            - Powers are not constitutionally protected
        - General Reasons: Ethinic cleavages and economic interest
            - Ex.
                - Requests for autonomy in Wales, Scotland, N. Ireland
                - Special economic zones in China
    - Federal -> Unitary
        - -> Asymmetric Federal System
        - Central government consolidates power
            - Constitutional powers for regional governments are taken away
        - General Reasons: Safety and National Security
            - Ex.
                - Terroism in Russia led to presidential appointments of district leaders
                    - district leaders aren't voted into office anymore
                - Mexico's war on drugs
