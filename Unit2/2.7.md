# Independent Legislature

## Learning Objectives
1. Explain how legislative powers are constrained by other institutions and/or processes, which can affect legislative independence


## Define It 
- Independent Legislature:
    - -> legislative bodies that have the power to make decisions and pass laws without interference or control from oter institutions or processes


## Why Should They Be Independent?
- Reasons:
    1. Shaping Legislation -> Legislatures have the ability to shape government
    2. Function Properly -> To function effectively, they should remain independent of other branches
        - Properly check the executive and judiciary
    3. Prevent Authoritative Creep -> Independence helps prevent any branch from becoming to authoritative
        - Remember that legislatures are often representatives of the people!
        - "Decree power" in many countries had allowed authoritative creep from the executive
