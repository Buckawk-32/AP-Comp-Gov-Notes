# WTO
1. It was made to regulate international trade, giving institutional framework for trade negotiations, allowing for trade to move as smoothly as possible.
2. WTO sets binding agreements on tarrifs, trade regulations, and fair competition, stopping trade wars and other conflicts from asrising.
3. WTO spefically focuses on international trade, making sure no disputes happen and trade rules are being followed
4. Some countries will prioritize their own domestic interest, implementing protectionist policies to retain the interest of lobbyist, etc.
5. There is a Dispute Settlement Body (DSB) in which they oversees and vote on disputes and WTO has monitors on their members
