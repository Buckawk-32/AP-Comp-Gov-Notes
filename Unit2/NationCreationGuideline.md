# Legislative Branch
> [!INFO]
> PURE DEMOCRACY in the Class of Body, because the School of Order only has 2000 students at any given time

- Bicameral
    - Class of Seniors
        - Ex. House of Lords
        - Make sure techinicalities of new policy is fesible
        - Since the Senior class is leaving soon and focusing on college, their vote would skew results away from public's stance
            - This creates a system where seniors pass laws through their voting that they won't have to see the consequences for
        - Thus it is better to put them into a regulatory position
        - They are also well educated to be in the Class of Seniors, as they have gone through 4 years of education (more than the other student body)

    - Class of Body
        - Ex. House of Commons
        - 9th, 10th, 11th grade
        - *PURE DEMOCRACY*
            - Each student has a vote in whatever policy is being decided
                - Referendums are issued out to the public for policy desicions
            - Each student who isn't a senior is and will have participate in the Class of Body
            - Due to the ciriculum, students will have a good understanding of civil society and politics

- Check on the Executive
    - Can do an appeal on executive orders of the executive branch


# Judicial Branch

> To be a justice, you must have a 5 in AP Comp Gov, and be in the Mock Trial / Debate Club

- *Minimum Requirement: AP Comp Gov students that all got an 5*
    - Before their senior year

- 2 Courts
    - Senior Court 
        - Holds judicial review
        - Holds Apellate Jurisdiction
        - Top members of Debate and Mock Trial Club is a requirement to be a justice in Senior Court
            - Members of 2 years at least
            - Top 10 speakers at least 5 times in competitions
            - 10 different unique competitions with competitive rankings
            - Have to get approval by the executive

    - Freshman Courts
        - Holds Original Jurisdiction 
        - Lower tier members of Debate and Mock Trial Club
            - At least a Member
            - Have to get approval by the Club presidents of both and the Executive

    - Common Law
        - Based of precedent set by Senior Court
        - Common Law is in refference to Executive orders to push Legislative-voted policies
