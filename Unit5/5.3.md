# Challenges from Globalization

## Learning Objectives:
1. Explain how globalization creates *challenges to regime sovereignty*

## 4 Key Challenges to National Sovereignty 
- 4 primary aspects of globalization can challenge a regime's sovereignty:
    1. Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) -> Investment in a country by indivudals or companies from another country, with the intention of pursuing personal interests in the company receiving the investment
    2. Cultural Influences -> Can sometimes promote a domestic backlash
    3. Enviromental & Health Issues -> increased economic development can cause economic degradation and poor health
    4. Political/Economic Pressure -> treat reversals, ecnomic sanctions, condemnation at international bodies (Ex. UN)
