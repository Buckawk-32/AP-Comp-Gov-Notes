# Political Stability

## Learning Objectives
1. Explain how internal actors influence and interact with state authority and either enhance or threaten stability


## What is Political Stability?
- -> preservation of:
    - an intact and smoothly functioning government or political system
- -> which avoids:
    - significant disruptions or changes over an extended duration


## Factors of Political Stability
1. Fight against corruption
2. Appropriate government response to challenges
    - Separatist violence
    - Crime and drug trafficking
    - Discrimination
    - Opposition groups

### Fight agaisnt corruption
- UK, Mexico, Nigeria
    - Freedom of Information Acts
        - More information for public and journalists
        - Even if it hurts the current government

- China
    - Xi's Purge
        - MILLIONS of officals arrested for corruption since 2013

### State Responses to Challenges
- Iran
    - Reserves seats in the Majles (legislature) for Christians, Jews, Women
   
- Mexico
    - Struggling to combat the cartels
    - High murder rate, kidnappings
    - Signs of INSTABILITY!

- Nigeria
    - Challenges from Boko Haram
        - Wants to estabilish an Islamic theocracy in the North


## Mass Protests - What to Do?
- China 
    - 1989 Tiananmen Square
        - 300 ~ 10,000 deaths
        - All discussion of political reform crushed
    - 2022 White Paper Protest

        - Had some sucess
            - Because the movement of holding up a blank white paper couldn't be considered protest, although it was sending a message that everyone knew

- Iran 
    - 2019 Green Movement
        - Against electoral fraud
        - Government cracked down harshly
    - 2023 Mahsa Amini Protests
        - Following the death of female student
