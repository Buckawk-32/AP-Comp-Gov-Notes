## Economic Liberalization
- -> When a state reduces its economic role and embraces free market policies
    1. Eliminating *tariffs*
    2. Eliminate *subsidies* (-> government financial support to businesses or individuals to reduce costs, encourage activity, or stabilize markets. They can be cash payments, tax breaks, or price supports)
    3. *Privatization*
    4. Opening up to *FDI*

- Prominent examples:
    - China's SEZs (Special Economic Zones)
    - Mexico opening up to NAFTA
    - UK under Thatcher (1980s) -> more privatization + deregulation of financial markets

## Why Liberalize?
- Goals:
    1. Solve *unemployment*
    2. Increase *productivity*
    3. Correct *trade imbalances* with other nations
    4. Shift from a *resource-based economy* to a *manufacturing economy*


## Expectation vs. Reality
- Harsh consequences of neoliberalism economic policies
    - Persistent political corruption
        - Neoliberalism gives more powers to private enterprises, which motivates corporation owners to gain even more power by infringing the laws and/or buying support from politicians
    - Exacerbation of socio-political tensions
        - Balance of economic freedoms vs. economic equality
    - Enviromental pollution
        - *Urban Sprawl* -> uncontrolled  or unplanned expansion of urban areas into surrounding rural land, often characterized by low density, car dependent development
            - Internal/domestic migration, as seen in:
                - Mexico (south -> north)
                - China (west -> east)
