# Political & Social Cleavages

## Learning Objectives:
1. Describe politically relevant *social cleavages*
2. Explain how political and *social cleavages* in course countries *affect citizen relationships and political stability*


## What are Cleavages?
- Cleavages
    - -> Internal divisons that structure society and may be based on class, ethnicity, religion, and/or territory
    - Social: differences in class, ethinicity, religion, and/or territory
    - Political: when social cleavages become politicized, the political cleavages then impact political culture and political behavior and values and cause tensions and conflicts in politics

    - 2 types:
        1. Coinciding Cleavages
            - -> When every dipute aligns the same groups against each other, these disputes are likely to be explosive
            - Deep divisions that may result in conflict if allowed to fester
        2. Cross-Cutting Cleavages
            - -> When divisions in a society split into many potential groups that may conflict on one issue but cooperate on another
            - Tend to keep social conflict to more moderate levels


## Why are they important?
- Shape the way certain groups interact with the government
    - Long-term effects on a regime and people


## Demands of Different Cleavages
1. Separatists
    - Seeks separation from the large group
    - Comes about when a group feels they are not protected by the state
        - US examples: Republic of Texas Movement, Nation of Islam, Lakota (Grat Sioux Nation)

2. Autonomy Seeking
    - Want more independence and representation
        - US Examples: Navajo and Cherokee Nations, most Puerto Ricans
        
- Note that, while *Ethinic Cleavages* are the most common and obvious, cleavages are not always along ethinic lines!
    - Urban vs. Rural
    - Class (UK)
    - Secular vs. Religious (India, France)
    - Generational
    - Ideaological (Taiwan, US)


## Iranian Cleavages & Responses
- Social Cleavage Example
    - Shiite majority vs:
        - Sunni Muslims
        - Zoroastrians
        - Christians & Jews

    - Persians (ethnicity) vs:
        - Azerbaijanis & Kurds

- Government Responses
    - Officially recognizes most minority religions
    - But oppresses the Baha i Faith
        - (Iran's version of Mormons)
        - Imprisoned & tortured
    - No clear rights for Sunnis
    - Ongoing fight with the Kurds

- Impact
    - Ongoing separatist movements
        - Kurdistan
        - Iranian Azerbaijan


## Mexican Cleavages & Responses
- Social Cleavages Example
    - Indigenous (Amerindian) vs. Mestizos
        - [Zapatista](/Unit3/3.5.md#mexico)
    - Regional -> North vs. South
    - Urban (70% of population) vs. rural
        - Rural = PRI supporters

- Government Responses
    - Increasing indigenous rights since 1994
    - Poverty reduction policies to help the South
        - [State Welfare Response](/Unit3/3.4.md#socialeconomic-equality-in-different-regimes)

- Impact
    - Some groups ask for more autonomy
    - Zapatistas marginalized


## Russian Cleavages & Responses
- Social Cleavages Example
    - Ethinic Reussians: 80% of populations
        - Slavic, Orthodox Christian
    - Everything Else
        - Tartar, Chechens, Ukrainians, etc.

- Government Responses
    - Wars to prevent separatism
    - Subvert local autonomy

- Impact
    - Continued separatist movements
    - Frequent terrorist attacks
        - [Moscow Concert Hall (2024)](/Unit3/3.5.md#russia)


## Nigerian Cleavages & Responses
- Social Cleavages Example
    - 250 ethinic groups
        - Igbo (SouthEast), Yoruba (SouthWest), Hausa (North)
    - North (muslim) vs. South (christian)

- Government Responses
    - 1999 Constitution provides [federalism](/Unit1/1.7.md#federal-system)
        - All powers not granted to the federal government are reserved to the states
    - Military used against separatist group
        - [Boko Haram](/Unit3/3.5.md#nigeria)
    >[!WARNING]
    > MAJOR PROBLEM: politicization of ethinic & religious differences

- Impact
    - More separatism than in Mexico
        - Divides are much deeper
        - More recent colonial history & unnatural borders
            - [Nigeria Political Culture](/Unit3/3.2.md#nigeria-political-culture-policy)


## Chinese Cleavages & Responses
- Social Cleavages Example
    - 55 recognized ethnic minorities
        - Uighurs in (NortWest), Tibetans in (SouthWest)
        - [Uighur Riots](/Unit3/3.5.md#china)
    - Urban vs. Rural

- Government Responses
    - Re-education/concentration camps
    - Rural development projects

- Impacts
    - Separatist Groups
        - XinJiang, Tibet, Hong Kong, Taiwan(?)
        - Limited effectiveness, however
        

## British Cleavages & Responses
- Social Cleavages Example
    - Ethnic/Regional
        - England, Wales, Scotland, North Ireland
    - Religious
        - Protestant vs. Catholic
    - Racial
        - Whites vs. Non-White Immigrants

- Government Responses
    - [Devolution](/Unit1/1.7.md#systemic-changes)
        - Local governments better suited to handle local issues

- Impacts
    - Autonomy seeking in smaller regions: Welsh, Isle of Wight, Shetlands
