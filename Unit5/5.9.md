# Impact of Natural Resources

## Learning Objectives:
1. Explain how natural resources affect political and economic development

## Impact of Natural Resources
- Important for all countries
    - Fuel development & industrialization
    - Type and abundance of them will affect economic growth and political stability

- Natural resource include:
    - Oil
    - Gas
    - Coal
    - Forests
    - Rare eath minerals
    - Etc.


## Resource Nationalization
- Nationalized in varying degrees in:
    - China, Nigeria, Russia, Iran, and Mexico

- Purpose:
    1. Provide revenue to the government
    2. Consolidate government control
        - Privatization of PEMEX in Mexico shows what happens when resources aren't nationalized
    3. Reduce political influence of foreign governments and MNCs
        - Nigeria -> MNCs pay for oil rents


## Rentier States
- -> countries that derive a significant part of their revenue from the rent or slae of natural resources
    - Reliance of single commodity as the largest source of revenue
    - Country profits from "rents" (excess profits from resource extraction), which are collected by the state and distributed through government spending, rather than being generated by a competitive, productive economy
        - Often leads to corruption
        - Politicians captialize on the commodity


## Russia as a Rentier State
- Shock therapy after 1991 resulted in massive privatization
    - Handful of people bought up vital industries for pennies on the dollar
    - Mostly oligarchs who challenged the system
        - Putin cracked down, forced the sale of Yukos (private firm) to Gazprom (nationalized form)

- Today, oil & gas = 40% of government revenue
    - Exports to Europe and China

- Benefits:
    - Can afford to keep taxes on citizens relatively low
    - Foreign policy leverage
        - Gas cutoff to Ukraine & western Europe
        - Pipeline to China to subvert western powers
- Drawbacks:
    - Vunerable to price shocks
    - Weakens the social contract
        - Taxation creates accountability between the government and the people
            - Weakens democratic accountability that allows for more centralized, authoritarian governance


## Nigeria as a Rentier State
- Pretroleum = over 90% of export earnings and about 50% ~ 70% of government revenue
- NNPC works with western MNCs to collect "rents"
- Allows the government to operate with limited accountability, leading to corruption, mismanagement, and weak public services
- Similar benefits and drawbacks as Russia


## Iran as a Rentier State
- Pre sanctions, oil = 50% of government revenue and the vast majority of export earnings
    - Dominanted by the National Iranian Oil Compnay (NIOC)
        - Controlled by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps
    - Funds:
        1. Domestic subsidies
        2. Military Programs
        3. Regional Proxy Forces (Hezbollah, Hamas, Houthis)
    
- Remember, this contributes to the brain drain
    - No economic opportunities outside of oil & gas


## OPEC
- Organization of the Petroluem Exporting Countries
    - Founded in 1960
    - 13 members, led by Saudi Arabia
    - Nigeria & Iran are members

- Controls prices by regulating production among member states
    - Reduce prodcution -> price increases
    - Increase production -> price falls

- Political tool to pressure rival economies
    - 1973 Oil Crisis


## Resource Curse
- Lack of economic diversification increases the dependence on a certain good
    - Concentration of voernment resources to develop this one profitable export
        - But at the expense of other industries
        - No incentive to modernize because the policy makers are all getting rich!
    - Severe revenue fluctuations
    - Overvaluation of currency and trade imbalances
    - Increased corruption
    - Growing rich-poor gap
