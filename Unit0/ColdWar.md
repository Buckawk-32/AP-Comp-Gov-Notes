## Ideological & Security Divide:
	US -> liberal democracy, free markets, indivdualism
	USSR -> one-party rule, state socialism, collective security
	Key Policies:
	- Truman Doctrine (1947): Containment of Communism
	- Marshall Plan (1948): $13B in aid to rebuild Western Europe
	- COMECON (1949): Soivet Economic alliance countering US aid
	Russia's Paranoia: Desire for buffer zones after repeated Western invasions (Also they saw these new policies as a prepration for invasion by the US)
