## Thomas Hobbes (1588 ~ 1679)
- Born in London
- Attended Oxford
- One of the founders of modern political philsophy
- Worldview shaped by the English Civil War, 30 years War
    - 1642 ~ 1651
- Traveled around Europe, observed other forms of government
    - Absolute monarchies in France, Austria, Russia, Prussia

### Hobbes' Politiccal Philosophy
- Through war and travels, Hobbes concluded:
    1. State of Nature (natural state of mankind) is a state of war
        - Man is selfish and brutish
    2. Way out of the State of Nature is a Social Contract
        - Agreed upon by the people to be governed by a government
    3. Ideal form of government is an absolute monarchy
        - Only form that can subvert mankind's natural state and create stability


## John Locke (1632 ~ 1704) 
- Born in England
- Attended Oxford
- Enlightenment thinker
- Father of "Classical Liberalism"
- Fled during the times of oppression after English Civil War
    - Went to France then to Holland
- Worldview shaped by the Glorious Revolution (1688)

### Locke's Political Philosophy
- 3 main points:
    1. State of Nature (natural state of mankind) is characterized by freedom and equality
        - People are born free and obligated to obey their creator
        - Government are necessary because some people do not obey
    2. People voluntarily give government some power through a social contract
        - Government in turn protect natural rights => Life, Liberity, and Property
    3. If the government fails to protect natural right, people are entitled to rebel and create a new one 

- Main englightenment influencer of both the American and French Revolutions!


# Executive Systems and Term Limit

## Learning Objectives:
1. Explain the structure, function, and change of executive leadership in course countries
2. Explain the advantages and disadvantages of executive term limits in course countries
3. Describe procedures for the removal of executive leadership by other institutions


## Essential Knowledge
- 2 General Principles of Executive Systems:
    1. Governments have executive institutions, including cheif executives and cabinets, that formulate, implement, and enforce policy through differenct methods and agencies
    2. The structure and function of political institutions reflect the allocation of power within a political system


## Executives
- Head of State
    - Highest representation of the state
    - Ceremoninal
    - Doesn't oversee day-to-day politics
    - Some foriegn policy powers

- Head of government
    - Chief executive
    - Formulates, implements, and executes policies
    - Helped by a cabinet and government agencies

- Parliamentary System: Seperate Heads of State & Gov
- Presidential System: Combined Heads of State & Gov

## Executive Term Limits: Advantages
- 3 clear advantages:
    1. Checks on Power
        - Precents the emergence of authoritarian leaders
        - Prevents a cult of personality from arising
    2. Focus on the Job
        - Governing > Campaingning and winning elections
    3. Innovation of ideas
        - Opportunities for new leaders, ideas, policies, and goals
        - Different generations and backgrounds have an opportunity
            - Without term limits, the executive's *incumbent advantage* (incumbent => the original person in power) is often too hard to overcome.


## Executive Term Limits: Disadvantages
- 7 clear disadvantages:
    1. Forcing good executive out without popular approval for doing so 
        - Politicians with high approval and good job ratings can do longer serve
    2. Insufficient Time to Implement Policy
        - Policy implementation take time
            - Is a 6 year term enough to enact meaninggul reforms??
    3. Impedes Policy Continuity
        - Chaning govenrment frequently leads to frequent ideological changes
        - Executive orders and major polic gets overturned frequently
    4. Weakens Accountability
        - The thinking that "they will be gone soon" leads to less oversight and accountability
    5. Lame Duck Period
        - -> Time after the election and before the inauguration
            - Nothing gets done
        - Wasted time = government ineffciency
    6. Lack of Executive Experience
        - Once an executive has enough experience to get things done, they are forced out
    7. Causes poorly designed policy
        - Lack of time + lack of experience = bad policies
