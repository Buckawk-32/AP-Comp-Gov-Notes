## Learning Objectives
1. Explain how government and regimes change


## General Principles of Power
- Rule 1:
    - Use of power is determined by democratic or authoritarian characteristics
        - UK/Mexico
            - High citizen participation, lower military participation
        - China/Iran
            - Low citizen participation, high military participation

- Rule 2:
    - Change in regime results in a change in the way a state is organized
        - All rules & institutions
        - Gradual or sudden
    - Change in government is a change in the people or parties
        - Frequent 
        - Peace or Violeance
            - If violence is implied it is most likely for a regime change
            - Ex. Violence causing Regime Change
                - French Revolution
            - Ex. Violence causing Government Change
                - Abraham Lincoln getting assinated


## Types of Regime Change 
- Coup d'etat (Type of Revolution)
    - Violent seizure of power
    - Typically "Top Down"
        - Powerful People at the top, changing other people
        - Other political people use force to force out their opponents
            - Can be a threat of force or the direct use of force
            - You can threaten a coup
    - Ex. Nigeria prior to 1999

- Revolution
    - Bottom up or Top down (Mao's Great Leap Forward, 1958 ~ 1962)
        - Most Revolutions are Bottom Up
        - But Mao's Great Leap Forward was Top Down
    - Can be violent (Russia 1917) or non-violent (East Germany 1989)
        

## Case Study Coups & Revolutions:

### UK
- English Civil War (1642 ~ 1651)
    - Roundheads vs Cavaliers
    - Result: Execution of King Charles I
        - A war agaisnt the Parliament vs Monarchy
            - Monarchy -> Charles I and the Cavaliers
            - Parliament -> Roundheads
        - Charles wasn't keen on giving up power, due to other countries having a abosulte monarchy
        - Parliament fought and won the war, getting power

- Glorious Revolution (1688)
    - (Catholic) King James II -> (Protestant) William and Mary
    - Result: Parliament given ultimate power over the monarchy 

### China
- Xinhai Revolution (1911)
    - Ended the Qing Dynasty
    - Result: Establish of the ROC

- Communist Revolution (1949)
    - Communists vs KouMingTang
    - Result: Establish of the PRC

- Great Leap Forward (1958)
    - Mao's attempt to force industrialization
    - ~ 50 million deaths
        - Top Down Revolution

- Cultural Revolution (1966 ~ 1976)
    - Attempt to return to "true socialism"
    - Also, purging enemies
    - ~ 2 million deaths
        - Top Down Revolution

### Iran
- Authoritarian -> Authoritarian (Still Regime Change)

- Coup against Prime Minister Mossadeq (1953)
    - Democratically elected
    - But tried to nationalize Iranian Oil

- Islamic Revolution - 1979
    - Overthrew the Shah (King)
        - He was favorable to the Americans to take Iranian Oil
        - He was also a dictator which oppressed the public
    - Led by Ayatollah Khomeini
        - Not to be confused with the current Ayatollah Khanmenei
    - Established the Islamic Republic

### Russia
- February Revolution (1917)
    - Overthrew the Tsar (emperor)
        - The Tsar/Romanov
    - Establised a Provisional Government

- October Revolution (1917)
    - Overthrew the Provisional Government
    - Established Lenin as head of government

- Russian Revolution (1918 ~ 1923)
    - Reds (communists) vs Whites (everyone else)
    - Established the USSR (Soviet Union)
    - ~ 10 million deaths

- Russian Coup attempt (1991)
    - Military tried to seize power and preserve the USSR
    - Lost. End of USSR. End of Cold War.
